# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ECDAT-X a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what
is best for the community, and show empathy toward other contributors. Harassment, trolling,
personal attacks, publishing private information, and other unprofessional conduct are prohibited.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Report incidents privately through the same
maintainer channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
